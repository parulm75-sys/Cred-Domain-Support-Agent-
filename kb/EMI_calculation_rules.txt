EMI calculation has 3 factors to be considered. We need monthly interest rate, time in months and the total amount owed.
Firstly, total interest is calculated for the duration by the formula rate*time*amount.
Then, the figure is added to the amount owed which is finally divided by the total duration of the loan.
Suppose, you have a loan amount of 100000 INR at interest rate of 1% monthly for 12 months, then your total interest would be 12000 INR.
Your total amount owing would be 112000 INR total, this will result in EMI of about 9333 INR per month.
