If any transaction seems to be a fraudulent transaction, then you can dispute it.
You need to fill all the information regarding the transaction.
The date, amount, merchant's name and the reason for which it seems to be fraudulent needs to be filled out in the application.
There are four possible reasons that can be mentioned. The reasons are: the transaction is not recognised, the amount is overcharged, the transaction was not authorised or Others under which you need to specify the reason.
Once the application is submitted, it will take 3-5 business days to review your request and decision will be emailed to you. The disputed amount will be held meanwhile.
