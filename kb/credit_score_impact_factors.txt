Credit score can be impacted by many factors which are as follows. The default credit score of 700 is given to every person whose age is 18 or above.
Every credit check brings down the credit score by 5 points. The outstanding loan amount on the person will have a negative impact. 
A higher loan-to-income ratio reduces the score further.
Every late bill payment will decrease the score by 30 points. Consecutive 6 months with no late bill payment will increase the score by 20 points.
