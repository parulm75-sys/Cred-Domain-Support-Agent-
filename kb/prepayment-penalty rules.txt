A prepayment penalty applies when a borrower repays part or all of a loan ahead of the scheduled repayment date.
No prepayment is permitted within the first six months of the loan term for any loan type.
After the lock-in period, borrowers who partially prepay will be charged 2% of the amount prepaid, while borrowers who close the loan entirely will be charged 3% of the outstanding principal at the time of closure.
Home loans are exempt from all prepayment penalties in accordance with regulatory guidelines.
All other loan types offered by the Bank, including personal loans, auto loans, education loans, and business loans, remain subject to the charges stated above.
