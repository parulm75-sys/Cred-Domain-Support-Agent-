Joint account rules are as follows.
To make a deposit, any one of the account holders can make a deposit.
To make a withdrawal, both parties are required to be present or at least the signature for both is required.
To apply for a loan, both account holders need to be physically present to apply.
To close a joint account, both account holders need to be physically present.
In case one of the account holder dies, it can be converted to an individual account by providing the death certificate and will be processed within a week. Meanwhile, all the assets will be on hold and cannot be withdrawn
