Credit card charges the customers as well as merchant at the same time. To use the credit card, there is 0.5% fee to the 
user and 2% fee to the merchant for every transaction. Interest is also charged to the balance that is not paid until the due date
which is calculated on the basis of simple interest of 1% every month that is added to the monthly statement. But there is no late payment fees.
There is also a 100 INR annual fee for the user.
